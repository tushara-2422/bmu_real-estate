Our mission

Real Estate, Minus the Noise

Build My Universe started in 2016 with a simple frustration: finding a home meant fake listings, pushy brokers and paperwork nobody explained. So we built the opposite: a team of advisors who visit every property, tell you the truth about it, and stay with you after the deal is done.

Today we work across six cities, but the rule hasn't changed: if we wouldn't live there ourselves, we won't list it.
