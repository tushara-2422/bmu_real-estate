Things people usually ask us

If your question isn't here, give us a call and ask.

Which parts of Bangalore do you cover?

Most of the city. A lot of our work is in Whitefield, Sarjapur Road and Bellandur in the east, Koramangala, HSR and JP Nagar in the south, Indiranagar and Sadashivanagar in the centre, and Hebbal, Yelahanka and Devanahalli up north. If you're looking somewhere else, ask anyway.

How do you check the paperwork?

Before we show you a home, our lawyer goes through the title, the khata, the encumbrance certificate and the layout approval. For new projects we also check the RERA registration. You get a short summary in plain English before you pay any token amount.

I work on the Outer Ring Road. Where should I live?

It depends which end. For Bellandur and Marathahalli, Sarjapur Road and HSR are good options. For Whitefield, living close by is usually worth it, or near a Purple Line station. We check the commute at peak hour before suggesting anything.

How much do you charge?

It depends on whether you're buying, renting or selling, and we'll tell you the exact fee on the first call. You don't pay anything until the deal is done.

Can I buy from outside India?

Yes, plenty of our clients do. We do video walkthroughs, help with the power of attorney and NRI home loans, and can look after the house once it's yours.

How soon can I move into a rental?

If the flat is furnished and the owner is ready, often within a week or two. That includes the rental agreement and police verification.
